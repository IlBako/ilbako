I'm Pier Francesco, a space engineer from Italy. I studied at Politecnico di Milano and spent four years in the GNC team of [Skyward Experimental Rocketry](https://skywarder.eu/). One of those years was I lead the GNC team and was responsible for the ground station on launch day. 
What I enjoy most is guidance and control on things that actually fly, and the launch operations around them.

My thesis code is in [Bachini-Thesis](https://github.com/IlBako/Bachini-Thesis): guidance and control for spacecraft flying close to asteroids. The rest is mostly things I build to learn, like an [8080 emulator](https://github.com/IlBako/Crabinvaders) and a [GPU ray tracer](https://github.com/IlBako/rusty-renderer), both in Rust.
